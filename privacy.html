Brain Stack

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Brain Stack Android app (package name com.pgarg.wordstackbuilder) handles your information. Brain Stack is published by pgargdevworks, referred to here as "we".

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The app shows optional video ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to provide those ads.

Information stored on your device

Brain Stack saves your scores, levels, coins, purchases made with in-game coins, skill ratings, streaks, achievements and settings on your device. This includes the daily reminder time if you choose one. We do not have access to this information.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings.

Advertising

Brain Stack uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. You can choose to watch a video ad to get extra tries or lives in some games.

The ad service starts when the app opens so that an ad is ready, which means data may be collected even if you never choose to watch an ad. Google's ad service collects and shares the following data:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your device's general location.
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to signed-in accounts.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. Ads may be personalized based on this data where permitted and according to your choices. We limit ads in Brain Stack to content rated for general audiences.

Google handles this data under its own policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your advertising choices

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: where required, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, then Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.
  • Personalized ads: you can manage your ad preferences in Google's My Ad Center.

Permissions

  • Internet and network access are used to load ads. The games themselves work offline.
  • Advertising ID is used by Google's ad service as described above.
  • Notifications are used only for the optional daily reminder, which you turn on in Settings.
  • Running after the device restarts lets the app restore your daily reminder.

Clipboard

When you tap to copy or share a result or challenge code, that text is placed on your device's clipboard. We do not receive it.

Children

Brain Stack is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete the game data stored on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. This permanently removes your progress unless it is restored from your own device backup.

We do not hold a copy of your game data or an account for you. Data collected by Google is kept and deleted according to Google's policies, and you can manage it through your Google Account if you are signed in.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not receive your game data, most requests about advertising data are best made through the Google tools linked above. You can also contact us for help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date shown at the top.

Contact us
